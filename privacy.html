Privacy Policy - SmartNest Publisher

Last updated: 26 November 2026

SmartNest Publisher is a private tool used only by SmartNest Finds UK to publish its own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to upload our own videos to our own channel and check their status.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): to store and remove review copies of videos that the tool itself created.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): to send notification emails to our own address. We do not read emails.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
